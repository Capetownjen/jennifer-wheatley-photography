About

Real People. Real Moments.

I photograph people. Not performances — the quiet glance between newlyweds, the way a father looks at his daughter, the warmth of a family gathered in golden light. After years working across Cape Town and beyond, I've built a practice around one simple truth: the best photographs are the ones that feel inevitable.

Whether it's an intimate beach ceremony, a corporate portrait, or a sprawling family reunion against the Twelve Apostles at sunset, I bring calm, craft, and an eye trained to find the extraordinary in the everyday.
