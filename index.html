About

Real People. Real Moments.

Born in Dublin and raised between South Africa and Ireland, my journey into photography began just after school with a brief detour into film. While I quickly discovered that the egos and pretence of that world weren’t for me, returning to Dublin to study photography felt like coming home.

I’m happiest when working with real people and their families. Whether I’m capturing a wedding or a family session, I know the most treasured images are often those quiet, in-between moments that unfold naturally. With a little gentle direction, the right tools and techniques, thoughtful planning, and a bit of friendly guest wrangling, I help even the most camera-shy members of your group relax and shine. Together, we’ll create the authentic, joyful photographs you’ll cherish for years to come.
